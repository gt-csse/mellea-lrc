# Root-identity recall gap audit, 2026-09-21

This audit compares saved artifacts with annotations. It makes no provider or model calls. The primary v33 run is the latest complete 26-filing body-resolution artifact examined here; it is a **saved-run diagnosis**, not a measurement of every subsequent code edit. The run did not supply a retrospective date to body search, so its score is not a leakage-controlled paper result.

## Corrected measurement

`primary-v33-root-identity-score.json` scores all 26 primary filings: 440 labeled roots, 367 gold-correct roots, 280 correct admissions, 2 wrong admissions, 87 missed correct roots, 76.29% admission recall, and 99.29% admission precision. The previously saved v33 score omitted 22 labeled roots from two filenames whose trailing hyphen was lost in the artifact name. `score_root_identity.py` now joins by the serialized `source_metadata.path`; its focused tests cover that join and duplicate detection. The older saved score must not be reused as a 26-filing result. On 2026-09-21, annotation 008-o41 was corrected from wrong to correct because its table-of-authorities occurrence cites the right case; a separate body occurrence contains the wrong name. Scores here and in the saved score JSON were recomputed after that correction.

`primary-v33-recall-misses.json` lists all 87 misses by annotation ID and span. They are 75 deferrals, 10 wrong rejections, and 2 roots never extracted. Sixty-six of the 75 deferred correct roots encountered a CourtListener 429 at body search or body fetch. Four of the other nine encountered a 500/502 or timeout. Seventeen deferred correct roots had a body-search route at or above the 20-candidate review limit; this overlaps provider failures. A failed request is not proof that a successful retry would admit the root.

Twenty-one deferred reporter roots reached case-level metadata but still needed evidence tying the *specific dated opinion* to the cited locator; 20 of those also encountered a 429 in body search or fetch. A same-name case record alone should not be admitted as the cited disposition.

## Recall work, ordered by expected general value

1. **Replay failed provider work from checkpoints.** Keep successful search/fetch results and retry only 429, 5xx, and timeout attempts with bounded pacing. On the same annotated roots, measure recovered admissions, new wrong admissions, request count, and provider failure rate. The 66 affected deferrals are an upper bound on this opportunity, not a predicted gain.
2. **Test the decision boundary on exact and metadata hits.** Two correct `Fed. App'x` roots (006-o69, 014-o03) were rejected even though exact lookup reached their records: the candidate check compares the display form of `Reporter` rather than its canonical form. Three more correct roots (018-o01, 022-o08, 022-o17) were rejected on CourtListener docket-court conflicts despite annotation evidence for the source court; verify the actual opinion court before a terminal verdict. Four other v33 wrong rejections came from broad name metadata searches with no reporter-locator evidence. Current `validation/roots.py` already defers these weak metadata hits, but this change has not been measured in a complete newer run.
3. **Improve missing case-name reads with a held-out ablation.** Saved extraction artifacts across primary, hallucination sets 1 and 2, and reliable high/low have 110, 70, 64, 101, and 248 present-locator occurrences with no parsed name. Root-only counts are 57, 49, 43, 45, and 111. Visible names can fail at `&` between party words or detached abbreviation punctuation immediately before a locator. Test one bounded, tokenizer-tolerant explicit-name reader; measure root-only name presence and normalized agreement, with exact spans and new false names as diagnostics. Hold out entire cases, including near-duplicate filings across sets. In primary v33, 13 of the reached missed gold-correct roots have `source.case_name=None`, but the current artifacts do not establish that the missing name caused their final verdicts.
4. **Test docket candidate generation without format-specific rules.** Saved five-set docket extraction found 233/244 annotated locator spans, compared with 3,096/3,103 for full reporter locators. Several docket misses were never proposed when adjacent to an already found locator; others were admitted only as a partial prefix, which then masked the rest from hunting. A candidate pass around existing full locators and a whole-token boundary check can be evaluated before changing admission rules. Reporter parsing is already near saturation.
5. **Rank broad body hits only after the above.** A total count of at least 20 currently stops review of that route, even when the first results are irrelevant. Test bounded ranking or pagination on the over-limit roots while retaining the distinction between a body mention and a self-identifying authority. Do not relax identity criteria merely because a search returned many documents.

## Annotation and precision checks

Annotation 022-o01's table-of-authorities `cited_as` originally crossed into the next citation and borrowed its `W.D. Va.` court and 2016 date. The root itself states `D. Ariz.` and no date. Its cited span and fields were corrected, while its gold identity label stayed correct. The derived JSONL and annotation/evidence checks were regenerated and passed.

Three reporter deferrals (015-o01, 018-o03, 011-o09) have gold notes that establish a case or docket but may not independently establish the exact WL/LEXIS serial. Review these before relaxing exact-locator admission. The 008-o41 root is now correctly labeled right; its later leaf's wrong name stays a separate occurrence-level concern.

Of the two remaining v33 wrong admissions, 015-o20 was a metadata-only same-name admission, a path current code now defers; 026-o33 is a one-letter name misspelling admitted by locator identity under the current strict gold rule. These deserve a targeted precision regression after the recall replay. Do not infer current precision from the saved artifact without rerunning changed stages.

Only primary and hallucination set 1 have saved full identity runs comparable enough for a diagnostic; they are from different code versions and provider conditions. The other three sets have extraction checkpoints but no complete identity score. This audit therefore does not establish cross-corpus identity generalization.
