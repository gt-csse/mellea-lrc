# Deferred name-led body discovery

These files are an unmodified snapshot from commit `e100120a`. They preserve the former case-name fallback and combined body reviewer for later design work. They are historical reference, not importable stages or part of the active package.

The active workflow searches fetched bodies for the cited reporter or docket locator and reviews only those grounded occurrences. A later name-led workflow needs its own retrieval, evidence contract, and judgment stage. A case-name hit alone cannot verify the original locator; it can lead to another record or citation that does.

Before adding that workflow, allow citation history to store a second body search for the same provider and a second review under distinct nodes. Keep each stage's saved evidence and retrospective cutoff independently recoverable.
